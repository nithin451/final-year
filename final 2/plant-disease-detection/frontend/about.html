About PlantGuard AI

AI-Powered Plant Leaf Disease Detection and Recommendation System using Convolutional Neural Networks

Project Overview

Plant diseases cause India to lose approximately 16–18% of annual crop yield, worth over ₹90,000 crore annually. Smallholder farmers in rural areas — especially in Andhra Pradesh — lack access to expert agronomists and often cannot diagnose diseases early enough to prevent crop loss.

PlantGuard AI solves this by using a fine-tuned MobileNetV2 Convolutional Neural Network trained on the PlantVillage dataset (87,000+ leaf images, 38 disease classes) to identify plant diseases from a single photograph with 92.4% accuracy.

Farmers can upload a leaf photo from their phone, get an instant AI diagnosis, and follow the recommended treatment — without needing an agronomist present.
